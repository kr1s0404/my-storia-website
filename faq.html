Questions & Answers

A Social Story is a short, personalized narrative developed by Carol Gray that describes a situation, skill, or concept using specific sentence types and ratios. They are an evidence-based tool used by therapists, educators, and families to help autistic individuals understand and prepare for everyday situations.

Mystoria is designed for both adults and children. Parents, caregivers, educators, and autistic individuals of all ages can use the app to create, customize, and read personalized social stories.

Yes. Story data is stored locally on your device by default. Cloud sync is optional and uses encrypted storage. We do not sell your data or use advertising trackers. You can delete all data at any time.

When you describe a situation, Mystoria uses a large language model to generate a Social Story that follows Carol Gray's methodology — proper sentence ratios, first-person perspective, positive framing, and age-appropriate language. You can also create stories manually without using the LLM feature.

Yes. Stories you've created are always available offline. Mystoria also supports on-device story generation, meaning you can create new stories without an internet connection.

Mystoria will be free to download and use when it launches, and will always be free.
