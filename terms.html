How generation allowances work

Allowances count cloud requests, not completed or approved stories. Story drafting, illustration planning, and cloud text revisions share the text allowance. Picture generation, reference-based generation, and regeneration share the picture allowance. A story with several pictures uses several picture requests.

Allowances reset on the first day of each calendar month at 00:00 UTC, including for annual plans. Unused allowance does not roll over and cannot be exchanged for money. The app displays remaining allowance and its reset date. Additional short-term rate limits may apply.

A request canceled before it is sent to the generation provider releases its reserved allowance. If it has been sent and its outcome is unknown, the allowance can remain reserved while the result is checked. Repeating an unchanged interrupted request uses the existing request identifier where recovery is available; contact support if a request remains unresolved. Do not assume an automatic refund or unlimited regeneration.

A cover that reuses an existing story picture does not require another picture-generation request. Local editing and rereading a saved story do not use cloud-generation allowance.

Saved stories and restoration

When Plus ends, your saved stories remain available for reading, editing, and export. Continued access to cloud generation requires an active subscription and remaining allowance.

Stories are saved on the device. Optional Cloud sync lets you retrieve uploaded stories on another device signed in to the same Mystoria account. Reading history and My world use a separate sync option on each device. Publishing a community template is an explicit action; private sync does not publish stories. Other people may keep copies they download even if you later withdraw a template. You can also use supported export/import features. Restore purchases verifies subscription access and is separate from story synchronization.

Use Restore purchases while signed in to the Mystoria account associated with your subscription. An active subscription is not intended to move between unrelated accounts. Contact support if you cannot access the original account.

Deleting your account or uninstalling Mystoria does not cancel your Apple subscription. Cancel it separately if you no longer want it to renew.
