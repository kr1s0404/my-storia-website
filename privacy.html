Your stories, and your choices

Mystoria helps adults and children create and read personalized stories and take part in short activities. This policy covers the Mystoria app and my-storia.com.

An adult should manage the account, purchases, and decisions about sending information to cloud services. Children can participate in stories and activities with their support. A child's details can be personal information even when an adult enters them.

Information we use

  • Account: your account identifier, sign-in information, and the email or name your sign-in provider shares. You can use Apple's Hide My Email where available.
  • Stories and activities: text, preferences, selected photos, cutouts, recordings, generated pictures, and reading or activity history that you choose to save.
  • Cloud requests: the brief, story text, image instructions, and reference images needed for the cloud action you request. Names or other details you include can be part of that request; we do not promise automatic removal of personal information.
  • Subscriptions: product and transaction identifiers, purchase status, renewal or expiration information, and generation usage. Apple processes payment details; Mystoria does not receive your complete payment-card number.
  • Technical information: network and service logs, app and OS version, and diagnostic or usage information processed by our service providers.
  • Support: your correspondence and any attachments you decide to send.

Local storage and planned cloud features

The current app stores your saved story library, personal photos and cutouts, and reading history on your device. Automatic story-library sync is not yet available in this build. This is the current implementation, not a commitment to local-only storage. Restoring purchases verifies subscription access; story sync is a separate feature.

Planned: account sync, sharing, and community templates

We are building cloud story storage so you can retrieve uploaded stories on another device using the same Mystoria account. The planned service uses Supabase for story records and access permissions, with cloud storage for associated pictures, photos, cutouts, or recordings included in an uploaded story. Syncing a private story will not publish it to the community.

Sharing a story is also planned to upload the selected story and its included media so the intended recipients can access it. Community publishing will be a separate, explicit action that makes a story available for other parents to download and use as a template. Creating a story, buying Plus, or syncing devices will not by itself publish that story.

These features are not yet available in the current app. Before launch, we will update this policy with the implemented storage providers, audience controls, retention, and deletion behavior. Review personal details and included media before sharing or publishing. Removing access to an original cannot guarantee deletion of copies someone has already downloaded.

Manual editing and supported on-device text generation do not send story content to OpenAI. Downloading the optional model still connects to its download host and uses network data. Sign-in, purchase verification, and other online services can also make network requests.

Adding a photo or cutout to a page locally does not by itself send that photo to OpenAI. Cloud illustration generation sends its instructions and any reference image used by that operation. If a cloud image-editing operation uses a photo you selected, that image must be transmitted for processing.

Local recordings are used for playback. Apple-provided speech and Personal Voice features are subject to device permissions and Apple's handling of those services. You can manage camera, microphone, and photo access in your device's Settings.

Services that help Mystoria work

Supabase

Account authentication, our cloud request gateway, subscription verification records, usage accounting, and temporary generation recovery data. Supabase is also part of the planned story-storage, same-account sync, and sharing service described above; those app flows are not yet available.

OpenAI

Cloud story and picture processing. The content needed for the requested generation is sent to the API. OpenAI states that API data is not used to train its models by default; provider retention and safety-review exceptions still apply. See OpenAI's data controls.

Apple and RevenueCat

Purchases, subscription status, restoration, and transaction support. RevenueCat associates purchases with your Mystoria account identifier and processes purchase and device-related information needed for its service.

Google Firebase

The app includes Firebase Analytics for technical and app-interaction information, including app-instance identifiers. Authentication is handled by Supabase, not Firebase. We do not intentionally include story text or personal photos in analytics events.

Website and downloads

GitHub Pages hosts this website. Google Apps Script handles the existing launch-notification form. Optional model downloads use their configured hosting provider, such as Hugging Face. These services receive the network information required to serve requests.

We use information to provide these features, maintain security, resolve errors, answer requests, and operate the service. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising. Providers may process information outside your country.

Children and sensitive information

Mystoria is intended for shared use with adult support. Enter only information that is needed for the story. A nickname and a general description are often enough; avoid full names, addresses, school identifiers, medical records, or other sensitive details when they are unnecessary.

Only include another person's information or photograph when you have the authority or permission to do so. Review both text and pictures before sharing a story with a child. Contact us if a child has submitted information without appropriate permission or you need help removing it.

Participation in a research study is separate from using the app or buying Plus. Research invitations on this website are optional and require their own study information and consent process.

Storage and retention

Local content remains on your device until you remove it. Exported copies, copies shared with others, and device backups can remain outside Mystoria's control. Account deletion on one device does not erase copies on another device.

For cloud generation, validated results may be temporarily stored to recover interrupted requests. Recovery is available for up to 24 hours; expired results are removed by scheduled cleanup. Usage records retain request identifiers, hashes, operation types, and timestamps to prevent duplicate charges and enforce allowances. They do not contain the original prompt or photo bytes.

Account and billing records are retained while needed to operate your account, reconcile purchases, prevent abuse, and meet applicable obligations. Apple, RevenueCat, OpenAI, and hosting providers also retain information under their own policies; deleting your Mystoria account does not automatically erase their independently retained records.

We use access controls and encrypted network connections, but cannot guarantee that any storage or transmission is completely secure.

Access, deletion, and contact

Use the app to edit or delete stories and export the stories you want to keep. To delete your account, open Account → Delete account. The app deletes the online account and then removes that account's saved data from the current device. If local removal is interrupted, the app offers a retry.

Deleting the app or your account does not cancel an Apple subscription. Manage subscriptions through your Apple subscription settings.

You can contact us to request access, correction, deletion, or other privacy rights available in your location. We may need to verify that a request concerns your account. Do not send a password or a child's private story to establish your identity.

Operated by Mystoria Inc.

Email support@my-storia.com for support, privacy questions, or a data request.

Website preferences and updates

The website stores your light/dark theme preference in your browser. If you submit the launch-notification form, your name, email, and optional research-interest selection are processed through Google Apps Script so the team can respond. You can request removal from that list.

We will update this page when our data practices change and identify the updated date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent before a material change.
