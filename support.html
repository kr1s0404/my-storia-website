Subscriptions and purchases

I paid, but Plus is not available.

Check your internet connection and sign in to the Mystoria account you used for the purchase. Open Settings → Mystoria Plus and choose Restore purchases. If Apple is still awaiting purchase approval, wait for that process to finish. Avoid purchasing again while confirmation is pending.

How do I cancel or request a refund?

Manage renewal in Apple's subscription settings. Canceling renewal normally keeps access until the paid period ends. Refund requests are handled by Apple. Deleting Mystoria or your account does not cancel your subscription.

Can I restore my stories on a different device?

Sign in to the same Mystoria account and turn on Cloud sync in Settings on both devices. Keep Mystoria open until the original device finishes syncing. Enable Include reading history and My world on each device if you also want reading positions, notes and reusable photos or cutouts. Restore purchases verifies subscription access; it is separate from story sync.

Stories, pictures, and allowances

A generation stopped or timed out.

Reconnect and retry the unchanged request using the app's recovery action where offered. A request already sent to the provider may remain reserved while its outcome is checked. Recovery of a completed server result is available for up to 24 hours. Contact us if it remains unresolved; include the error and request identifier if shown.

Why did one story use more than one credit?

Allowances count requests. A story draft and separate illustration plan use text requests, while each generated or regenerated picture uses a picture request. Editing locally, reading, and reusing an image for the cover do not use those allowances. See the allowance details.

A character looks different on another page.

Review the story and its character details, then regenerate the affected picture if you choose. Regeneration uses picture allowance. You can also use your own photo or cutout locally. Image consistency is not guaranteed.

Will syncing a story publish it to the community?

No. Account sync is private. Publishing a community template is a separate action that uploads the public copy you review, including any pictures you choose to include. It becomes available in Discover after the basic text checks. File exports go to the destination you select. Review personal details before sharing; downloaded copies cannot reliably be recalled.

The on-device model will not download or run.

Check your connection and available storage. On-device generation is only available on supported devices and needs a model download first. You can continue reading or editing saved stories without downloading the model.

Your account and data

To delete your account, open Settings → Delete account in Mystoria. Export anything you want to keep first. Account deletion removes the online account and then its local data from the current device. If the local step fails, use Retry removing local data.

Deletion does not cancel an Apple subscription or erase exported copies, backups, or files on other devices. For data-access or deletion questions, contact us using the details above. Read the Privacy Policy for the data flow and retention details.
